How Echo works · Example walkthrough

Help your team solve outages with less searching.

When a service stops working, teams lose time digging through old tickets, chats, and postmortems. Echo brings the closest past incident and its verified fix into one place—so responders can check the right things sooner and avoid repeating mistakes.

About this example: The incident and response below are fictional examples written for this walkthrough. They were not provided by you or your company. The demo memory bank contains four sample incident reports; Hindsight may break each report into smaller searchable facts, which is why the earlier screen showed so many lines.

A simple incident flow

From a customer problem to a better next response.

Here is a prepared example of what a responder could ask and how Echo could help. This walkthrough is written in advance; it does not send a request to Hindsight.

01

Describe what people see

A responder reports the service, the customer impact, and what changed recently.

Example prompt“Online checkout became very slow after this morning’s update. Some customers see an error. What should we check first?”
02

Find a similar past case

Echo searches the team’s incident history and brings back the closest matching case.

Fictional example · INC-1042A previous checkout slowdown followed an update. A repeated call to a slow tax service kept the service busy. Reverting that update restored checkout.
03

Get plain next steps

Echo explains what is known, what is only a possibility, and what the team can safely check.

Example answer“This looks similar to the earlier checkout issue, but the cause is not confirmed. Compare the latest update with the previous one and check whether a slow outside service is holding up checkout. Don’t restart the database or change customer data without approval.”
04

Save only what is verified

After the team confirms the cause and recovery, an operator can save the outcome and prevention step. The next response starts with better context.

Human confirmation requiredEcho never treats an old fix as permission to change a live system. The operator checks the resolution before it becomes a new memory.

What real problem does Echo solve?

During an outage, important lessons are often buried in old documents and conversations. Echo helps the on-call team find relevant history quickly, understand what worked before, and keep the verified outcome for next time. To use it with a real team, the memory bank must first be populated with that team’s approved incident reports.
